Setup

Three steps, no deposit to us

Your USDC never leaves your Hyperliquid account. The bot gets a key that can trade it and nothing else.

  1. STEP 01

    Connect your wallet

    MetaMask, Rabby, WalletConnect or a hardware device — the same wallet you already use on Hyperliquid. No account, no email.

  2. STEP 02

    Approve an API wallet

    One signature creates a named agent wallet with an expiry you choose. It can place and cancel orders — it cannot withdraw.

  3. STEP 03

    Pick a strategy

    Set the market, the leverage and the size cap. The bot works the book from there and stops the moment you revoke.

    Access

    What the bot's key can and cannot do

    Hyperliquid lets you approve an API wallet — a named key that acts for your account inside the exchange, with a limit on what it may touch.

    The API wallet can

    • 01Place, modify and cancel orders on the markets you enable
    • 02Open and close positions within the size and leverage you set
    • 03Run on a schedule you choose, with an expiry date on the approval

    It cannot

    • 01Withdraw USDC or move funds off Hyperliquid
    • 02Send your balance to another address

    FAQ

    Short answers

    What is a Hyperliquid trading bot?

    Software that trades perps on Hyperliquid for you. It watches the order book and funding, then places orders through an API wallet you approved — your USDC stays in your own Hyperliquid account the whole time.

    Does the bot hold my funds?

    No. There is no deposit address here. You fund your Hyperliquid account as usual, and the bot only holds a trade-only key that cannot withdraw or transfer your balance out.

    How do I stop it?

    Pause halts new orders immediately. Revoking the API wallet ends the bot's access entirely — after that it cannot place a single order, with no request to us.

    Can I lose money?

    Yes. Perps are leveraged: a move against a position is amplified, and past a point it liquidates. Size caps and stops limit how large a mistake gets — they cannot rule one out.
